# Journal

This journal is the provenance of the project in two parts, a register of the decisions that currently hold and a short entry per session since 2026-09-01. Superseded decisions, lapsed decisions and the full session narratives of February to September 2026 live in [journal-archive.md](journal-archive.md). An E number keeps its decision forever, it is never reassigned, never merged and never deleted, because it is the address of that decision in tests, scripts, vocabulary and knowledge base.

### Modelling guard rails

Cross-cutting principles that bind every model decision and are held by the tests ([testing.md](testing.md)).

- Spec-first. Every new property or class stands first in [data.md](data.md), then in the formal vocabulary [`../vocab/m3gim.ttl`](../vocab/m3gim.ttl), then in a test, then in the pipeline (E-133).
- Q-IDs never literal. Wikidata references arise from index or reconciliation alone, never as raw value text.
- Verify external terms, do not extrapolate. Every `rico:` and `agrelon:` term is attested against the official term list, the ICA-EGAD CSV and the RDF of the German National Library, before it enters spec or pipeline. Terms plausibly derived from a naming convention are the source of error (E-103, E-104).
- Do not invent confidence. No guessed value. The decimal dating confidence projected from `datierungsevidenz` was removed entirely (E-106), and `m3gim:qualityConfidence` of the data quality flags stays unfilled for the same reason (E-102). A dating provenance would return as a categorical value, not as a number.
- `hasAssociatedAgent` is object or list. With exactly one agent a bare object, and every consumer carries both forms (E-31).
- Namespace hygiene. `m3gim-role:` is a ConceptScheme for values and no entity namespace, and `hasStageRole` hangs on the performance, not on the record.
- Aboutness is not containment. A document reference is `scopeAndContent`, not `hasOrHadSubject` (E-101).
- Vocabulary coupling. Every new event role and role stands in the frontend and test vocabulary, otherwise the vocabulary tests break when it goes live.
