# Design

## Stance

The interface supports research through readable source evidence, explicit coverage and stable navigation (E-156). Gaps and duplicates remain visible; the cataloguing team uses the separate pipeline reports for source correction. The interface strings are German, the code and this document are English. Four rules govern every view.

1. Explanation comes from structure, symbols and tooltips. Standing explanatory text is the antipattern, counts live in the controls that carry them, captions and breakdowns live in tooltips. The tooltip is the preview, the detail column or the inline detail is the detail.
2. One shared search and filter strip stand above each research view. The collapsible left sidebar holds the time window and complete facet inventories. The Verknüpfung section stays open. Views use the shared document cut and make applying a selected aggregate an explicit action. Chronik, Netzwerk, Indizes, Orte and Dashboard share the selection detail component; its independent scroll exposes complete evidence while preserving local navigation (E-296).
3. Nothing is hidden silently. The application opens on the full result set (E-253), no cap and no threshold removes an entity from a picture (E-257), a count stands beside every facet value, and a data point a view cannot show is named beside the view with the reason.
4. Colour is spent on meaning only. The four content families, the accent as the single interactive colour, red for error and green for a Wikidata match are the whole vocabulary. A surface never takes the accent and an interactive state never takes a warm tone.

## Tokens

`docs/css/variables.css` holds every token and is the only source for the view stylesheets. Surfaces are warm and the accent is cool, and the two never mix (E-186). `--surface` is the white work area, the only ground content stands on, `--surface-2` the cream of the sidebar, `--surface-3` the parchment step of hover and keyboard cursor. `--line`, `--line-soft` and `--line-strong` are warm greys, so a hairline carries a boundary and no colour. `--accent` is the KUG blue of brand band, link, active tab, focus ring and selection outline, with `--accent-light`, `--accent-dark` and `--accent-soft` beside it. Text runs on `--color-text-primary`, `--color-text-secondary` and `--color-text-tertiary`, all warm, with `--color-text-inverse` on the band and `--color-absent` for a missing value. Exactly two semantic colours exist, `--color-error` for errors and data-quality flags and `--color-match` for a Wikidata match.

The four content families are the four entity kinds, `--chip-c-person-text`, `--chip-c-institution-text`, `--chip-c-ort-text` and `--chip-c-werk-text`, each at least 4,5 to 1 against white and separated in hue and lightness. The colour appears as the family icon of a Bestand row, as the dot at a block title of the detail, at the title of the four entity facets and at the head of a register (E-171, E-212, E-230). Chips stay neutral on `--chip-c-neutral-border`, so the legend arises from proximity, and `--swatch-color` stays transparent until a legend sets it. The categorical set `--cat-1` to `--cat-6` remains available through the mobility-perspective aliases `--color-sicht-*`. The Chronik uses the four family icons and neutral source marks.

Typography carries the kind of a value (E-187). `--font-mono` is reserved for identifiers, that is signature, folio, term id, Q-ID, `--font-title` is the serif of the word mark and the info-page headings, and everything else runs in `--font-ui` with tabular figures in number columns. Sizes run from `--text-2xs`, the half step of sidebar and legend counts, to `--text-3xl`, with `--weight-*` and `--leading-*` beside them. Spacing is an eight pixel grid, `--space-1` to `--space-16`, with `--space-1-5` for dense rows. The frame stands in `--view-sidebar-w`, `--brand-band-height` as `--top-bar-height`, `--footer-height` and `--table-head-height`, the last a token because two sticky layers stack on it. `--border-radius`, the two transitions and the three shadows close the set.

## Components

Sidebar and search. `ui/sidebar.js` composes the fixed-order filter column and centrally mounts one search toolbar in each research view. Typing supplies actions without changing the cut. The first, visibly selected action searches the draft as text and shows its prospective document count; subsequent actions explicitly filter by person, institution, work, place or document identity. Enter follows the highlighted keyboard selection. Hover has a weaker appearance and cannot change that selection. Opening a register, map position or full document remains a separate action that preserves the cut. Suggestions and facet lists expose their complete remainders. There are no additional local text searches. The result line remains the root of the document-type tree. Below 1200 viewport pixels the sidebar starts collapsed behind the labelled Filter control. Dokumenttyp precedes the entity facets, followed by Verknüpfung. Active chips identify their own condition, for example `Text: „Zürich“` and `Ort: Zürich`. A generic link-kind chip is omitted while a same-family entity filter implies it; its stored condition becomes visible again when the entity filter is removed. Filter undo/redo stays in the shared toolbar. Netzwerk and Chronik retain their upper-right legends independently of selection (E-299).

Workspace bounds. The shared shell uses the height remaining between the top band and footer. Each view keeps its drawing or list within that area and scrolls complete content internally. Below 900 pixels, an open sidebar and the work area share the available height; collapsing it returns the full height to the view. Compact selects and text actions use common control styles, with local dimensions retained (E-297).

Facet tree. Dokumenttyp shows its hierarchy. A selected broader term includes its descendants and marks them with a muted implied check. Selection, hover and keyboard focus retain separate appearances; the chevron exposes `aria-expanded`. Entity facets open bounded option lists with a complete expandable remainder. Every inventory value remains reachable, including a zero under the current conditions. Roles without a display label use an explicit fallback; missing roles remain a selectable category. Counts for a bound entity/role preserve the other half when evaluating candidate values. New entity-plus-role selections bind both to the same recorded entry regardless of selection order. Legacy unbound URLs remain labelled document co-occurrence and offer explicit conversion.

Chips. Semantic data points retain their uppercase role and value. Static parts, dates and amounts are selectable text with a transparent background and border. An action has an outlined container and an underlined value: a funnel marks filtering, an arrow marks navigation. The native action button and the external Wikidata link are separate keyboard targets. Entity filters follow the recorded class; CorporateBody and Group use the institution facet, and an unsupported class stays static. Derived values keep their dotted provenance mark (E-293). In the filter strip the removable outline chips remain grouped per facet (E-204).

Marks and family symbols. Four line icons in `docs/js/ui/family-icons.js` name person, institution, place and work, drawn in the family colour through `currentColor` and always `aria-hidden`, the meaning carried by tooltip and accessible name of the cell. Evidence against absence is a matter of stroke weight and colour. A quality flag of the model shows one neutral sign whose tooltip quotes the `rico:generalDescription` unchanged (E-222, E-243). Everything derived, inherited or supplemented carries exactly one mark, the dotted underline in secondary colour with a tooltip beginning "ergänzt: " (E-216), while absence stays italic in the absence colour.

Tooltips. The application renders `data-tip` in one fixed overlay attached to the page body, outside the columns of record details. This prevents a long tooltip from splitting between columns. `data-tip-pos` chooses the preferred placement, which is kept inside the viewport. Hover and keyboard focus select the closest tooltip-bearing element, including a Wikidata link nested in a chip. The overlay is linked through `aria-describedby` and closes on Escape, click, scroll, resize or departure from its trigger. Static information pages retain the CSS fallback, with `data-tip-wrap` for multiline content. Native `title` exists only on SVG drawings (E-36). One hover shows exactly one tooltip (E-90), and an icon button carries an `aria-label` beside it (E-210).

Source references. The interface identifies evidence by archival record and signature. Technical sheet, row and data-point labels are absent from chips, grouped dates and register tooltips (E-285). Their provenance remains in the dataset, exports and cataloguing reports.

Coverage line. The Dokumenttyp root row in the shared sidebar states the selected record count against the linked application basis. Its accessible tooltip gives the export date and view scope. A view accounts separately for evidence it can display, such as places with coordinates. Whole-fonds counts and unlinked records belong to the quality snapshot; the interface count does not measure completeness of the fonds.

The shared strip offers one `Filter löschen` action. Compact undo and redo arrows have explicit accessible names and tooltips describing the last filter change. Panel-only reset stays under `Darstellung`; it leaves the shared filter intact (E-300).

Detail column. Chronik, Netzwerk, Indizes, Orte and Dashboard share `ui/selection-detail.js` and its stylesheet (E-289). The component owns the heading, context label, optional subtitle and return action, close control, placement and focus. This complete header stays visible during scrolling. The view supplies its evidence content and selection semantics. Before selection the drawing uses the available width; a selection opens a 300-pixel right column only when the work area is at least 900 pixels wide. Narrower work areas use the same content in a native modal dialog. An overview shows identity, counts, necessary qualifications and the next available actions. Supporting lists open through labelled native disclosures or an explicit source-list state, and retain all entries. Escape and the close control restore focus to the originating selection; nested Chronik and place evidence offer a return to their preceding overview. The legend stays at the upper right independently. Selection changes horizontal space but preserves chronological y-coordinates and scroll position. Network selection preserves the existing node coordinates and zoom without another simulation. Register selection preserves list height and scroll position. A hidden view cannot open its retained panel as a modal over another tab (E-293, E-299).

Inline detail. The detail a Bestand row opens carries its own full title, no close control and no bundled source lines. It is closed from the row that opened it, by its chevron, by a click outside its title text and by Escape. Its head groups the full Signatur and the complete selectable title on the left, with the title below the signature in semibold. Type, date, language, extent and processing status form a compact group on the right with left-aligned text; on narrow screens this group follows below. Paging and Korb remain separate controls. The head takes focus when the detail opens without entering the tab sequence. When paging through a Folio, signature, individual title and metadata change together while the collection title remains in the table row (E-286). The record signature identifies the evidence, and the administrative fields stay in the collapsible foot. Active row and detail row are one bracket with a continuous left accent bar, inset by the table's cell padding (12 pixels, 8 in the narrow layout). The title remains complete and selectable in the row; marking or double-clicking its text preserves the open state. Expanded record and convolute titles wrap and use semibold weight. Collapsed titles use visual ellipsis while retaining the complete text for copying. Manual opening, switching and closing preserve the activated row's viewport position within the available scroll range. Opening a record or Folio page updates the document address; closing clears that selection. A direct link opens its convolute and places the addressed title immediately below the sticky column and convolute heads. Arrival is immediate. Short lists receive only the trailing scroll space needed to reach this position; filtered lists use the column head alone.

Register row. A native button containing the chevron, name and short enrichment opens the shared detail. Composer names carry the visible label Komponist. Document, network, map and Wikidata actions stand separately on the right; the evidence span gives way on narrow lists and remains available in the detail. The document link carries the complete filter in its href and preserves normal middle/modifier-click behaviour. Keyboard activation of another row action cannot toggle the detail (E-293, superseding the row-control and layout portions of E-252 and E-270).

Tabs. Seven tabs in three groups, Material, Perspektiven and Werkzeug, in one KUG blue band that also carries word mark and info links (E-160, E-185, E-196). Groups are set apart by spacing and a short hairline, never by a written label. The active tab takes white on a slightly lightened face with the white underline pushed into the band (E-211). The brand block takes the width of the sidebar column, so the first tab starts at the left edge of the work area, and between 900 and 1365 pixels the band keeps all seven labels by stepping padding and gaps down (E-244). The first click on the Indizes tab opens the register menu, which names the four registers with their family symbol and marks the chosen one with `aria-checked` (E-230).

Footer. One fixed row, neutral and without accent, in two groups, the KUG mark with the imprint on the left, repository, method, DHCraft and the CC BY licence on the right, each behind an equally sized mark (E-209), the explanations in the tooltips. `scripts/_site_html.py` supplies this row and the common page metadata/navigation; `scripts/sync-site-html.py` synchronizes the five pages independently of model generation (E-297).

## Views

Bestand. The records in archival order, convolutes as group heads with their children in signature order (E-82, E-203). A frameless record table over the full width, rows white on a warm hairline, the tinted tone only on hover, column head and parked open convolute head (E-191, E-198). A convolute opens closed, its chevron unfolds it and parks the head under the column head, and a direct link to a child opens its convolute (E-175, E-206). Arrow keys, Enter, space and Escape run through the heads (E-214). The head carries signature, title and date span, folded also the type distribution as small chips, with its numbers and its cataloguing state in the tooltip of the title (E-190, E-197). An object row carries an indented chevron for its inline detail with `aria-expanded`, shows the folio number without prefix, leaves the date empty where it equals the convolute, and shows the family icons of the entities it names with their counts (E-212, E-215, E-217). There is no sorting, because a hierarchy does not order flat.

Indizes. One register per page, selected in the tab menu, with count/alphabet sorting in the head (E-226, E-230, E-231). The list has one fixed-height row per entity. The shared detail separates properties, roles in documents, recorded relationships, stage parts and Im selben Dokument genannt. It previews three document roles; the complete role remainder and initially closed co-mention section expand in place. Stage parts remain unassigned unless a source statement explicitly binds them. Index information is identified by its own source; it is never presented as a binding attested by every document mentioning the work. Relationship groups expose every attesting document in the current cut. General document browsing uses the row link to Bestand; Netzwerk and Karte remain explicit view actions. Typed register and map navigation preserves the shared document filter and the recorded identity family (E-296, E-299).

Chronik. A vertical rail orders natural calendar groups with readable lanes for sources, places, persons, works and institutions (E-290, E-291). Scrolling down advances in time. The scale control chooses decades, years, months or days; each occupied calendar unit has one anchor and stable reading space. The sticky lane head keeps the current year visible, or identifies the current decade or folded year range. Empty stretches collapse to explicitly labelled, expandable folds, including inside context bands. The surface is therefore piecewise scaled, with no claim of one global pixels-per-year ratio. The initial position is the year with the most document-date references. Year navigation and scale changes preserve the shared document cut; a scale change preserves the current time anchor. No repeated title, totals line or introductory paragraph occupies the work area.

Lanes preview three named entries, with complete lists in the shared detail component. Source counts deduplicate record identities within a group; their original date contexts remain distinct in the detail. One or two distinct datings retain their original text. Longer previews count distinct dating values and summarize the first and last calendar months of precise values, for example `25 Datierungen · Jänner–Dezember 1963`. This span describes the dated values, without asserting continuous activity. Recorded ranges, qualified boundaries, approximate values, year-only precision and unclear dates receive separate counts; summaries remain unclipped. Full original datings are available by selecting the source. Solid and dotted source borders distinguish document dates and dated statements, as shown in the upper-right legend. A broad mentioned range contributes its start group without opening the intervening years. At finer scales, ranges and values of coarser precision remain accessible through Gröbere Zeitangaben; they do not acquire fabricated exact dates. Invalid or reversed values remain accessible through Datierung prüfen, and undated context through Ohne Datum.

The Chronik displays source dates and source statements only (E-301). Editorial biographical phases remain in the research documentation and contribute no timeline bands or phase controls.

The source and entity lanes wrap into two rows on medium hosts and below sources on phones. Every occupied group has the same height for the current corpus, scale and host width. That height follows the tallest rendered preview plus reading space, with the possible detail column accounted for before selection. A dense group therefore cannot overlap its neighbour, and sparse groups do not independently compress time. Selection opens the shared Chronik/Netzwerk detail column or narrow-screen dialog, with complete lists and back navigation. The calendar anchors retain their positions during selection; nothing unfolds inside the timeline. Selecting a source highlights its rendered entity connections across lanes. Typed parts remain separate from works.

Document dates and dated statements retain their own recorded values. Place and date combine only within the same annotation; a dated performance supplies only its own work, performers and parts. Other entity mentions and undated annotations stay in the document context, labelled `In der Quelle genannt`, with their original evidence date and provenance retained. Mixed groups explicitly identify both dated statements and source mentions. The shared time filter selects documents by their recorded document date with its precision and bounds. Content dates remain separate statements and do not replace a document date. Source-specific details restrict entity evidence to that source, show dating roles and caveats, and link into Bestand. Entity selection highlights the same identity where rendered, without filtering or inventing a shared performance, presence or journey.

Orte. A collapsible 264-pixel navigator lists grouped places and distinct document counts, including places without coordinates. The central map takes the remaining work area. Narrow hosts initially collapse the navigator; its labelled control exposes the complete list. Sorting belongs to the view head. The common search supplies place focus and typed filters; detailed roles live in the shared right-hand selection panel.

Explicit place selection centres its map point after the detail panel has settled, including a point already visible in the viewport. It retains a useful regional zoom and any stronger user zoom. Resizing or closing details does not refit the map. Selection leaves the document cut unchanged. The overview shows counts once, dated-statement coverage, coordinate provenance and the three most frequent roles, with the remaining roles expandable. Quellen ansehen opens the complete document-grouped source list; selecting a role opens that list restricted locally to the role. Each document discloses its independently recorded evidence with the role, original place wording, statement date, document date and source qualifications. Matching bound-query witnesses are marked within the complete qualifying-document context. The source list offers a return to the place overview. Filtering to the place and opening it in Chronik remain separate actions. Co-mentioned works, persons and institutions start closed and retain their complete supporting documents and interpretation limit (E-299).

Uniform map points identify grouped places; counts remain in the navigator and tooltip. Arrow navigation and the complete list provide keyboard access. Fit-all and viewport coverage controls describe the eligible points. Missing geometry leaves the list usable. Coordinates and country values must belong to the concrete source mention or its admitted authority reference; a shared name cannot supply another mention's missing geography. Notes distinguish supplied and absent coordinates. The shared time window selects documents by their recorded document date, which cannot fill an absent statement date. Coordinates and co-mentioned entities cannot establish a person's presence or journey.

Netzwerk. The co-mention network of the result set, in two forms of the same data, the overview and the neighbourhood after a click (E-268). Nodes are the actors named at the documents of the cut and the records that link them, edges are the mentions, and the creator of the fonds is no node (E-256). No cap and no threshold removes a node. The view brings two controls of its own into the shared sidebar, the first taking the record nodes away so that the person projection remains, the second fading the actors that carry a single record, and neither of them removes anything (E-257). Actors are drawn in one quiet tone, records as small squares in the accent, highlight is the only strong colour, and labels appear on highlight and from a zoom level. The edge layer is drawn on a canvas with the nodes as SVG above it, because the projection of the unfiltered fonds carries more edges than SVG can highlight without stalling. A click keeps the layout still, highlights the neighbourhood, fades the rest and opens the detail column, and the GEXF export writes exactly the projection that is drawn (E-259). In the two-mode network the neighbourhood of an actor runs two steps, the records of the actor and the actors standing at those records, the second step paler than the first, while a record node and the whole projection mark one step (E-276). Visual acceptance checks that the layout avoids a lattice and uses the available area, record nodes remain distinguishable at their small size, relation marks are readable, the second neighbourhood step is perceptible, and labels remain legible in the dense centre. The layout spreads into the aspect ratio of the drawing area instead of settling into a round blob, a bounded non-uniform fit takes the rest, and a few relaxation passes push apart only the pairs that touch, so the spacing stays irregular and no lattice forms (E-274).

Network neighbours and supporting sources open in separate disclosures. Evidence for a mentioned entry includes every supporting document of the current cut; projected-edge evidence includes every shared document. Each source remains individually reachable in Bestand (E-299).

Dashboard. The `statistik` route opens two independently configurable panels, initially document-type treemap and document-type/work matrix. An upper view-pair bar offers `Dokumenttypen + Werke`, `Zeit + Orte` and `Ortsrollen + Karte`; any independent configuration remains possible and is labelled `Freie Kombination`. A preset changes both panel addresses together while retaining filters, selection and comparison reference. Chart choice, dimensions and counting measure stay visible in each panel head; secondary settings and reset open under `Darstellung`, aggregate downloads under `Export`. Escape closes an open menu and returns focus to its trigger. Each panel is a named region with a hidden heading that follows its visualization. Available views include treemap, matrix, document-date time distribution, place-role Sankey, UpSet intersections, A/B comparison and the reused point map. The actual remaining container width determines whether panels sit side by side or stack (E-300).

The treemap partitions documents into descendant categories, direct-parent residuals and missing type. Breadcrumbs expose hierarchy. Matrix cells count distinct documents and identify the source binding or document co-mention they represent; row shares use a named document denominator. Axis focus and complete row/column navigation change presentation only. The time view places exact document years on a continuous axis. Qualified and interval dates retain their original values in separately selectable aggregates, including in the stacked view; they never enter an exact-year bin. Undated documents remain explicit. A local brush or numeric range becomes a common time filter only through its labelled apply action. Sankey bands count source statements across place-related dimensions and preserve the same source-reference set at each stage. Their geometry carries no travel interpretation. UpSet makes inclusive and exclusive set conditions explicit. Comparison names A and B denominators and overlapping documents and retains reference A while B changes.

Selecting a mark opens one dashboard-wide shared detail and highlights matching document IDs in both panels. Matrix cells use a contrasting contour while retaining the quantitative fill. Local panel changes preserve that selection; if its original control is replaced, closing the detail returns focus to the surviving originating panel. Additional marks form an explicit document union; each can be removed. Selection alone changes neither the filter nor the Korb. Applying the selection, adding its deduplicated sources to the Korb, opening a full record and downloading evidence are separate actions. The overview retains counts and qualifications for co-mention and unresolved assignment. Filter witnesses, when present, open under `Warum passen diese Dokumente zum Filter?`; mark evidence opens under `Belege für diese Auswahl`, supporting documents under `Quellen ansehen`. Positive provenance boilerplate is omitted from individual rows; missing original source positions remain explicit and exports retain the complete evidence. Every drawing has a complete native evidence-list equivalent. Zero, missing and remainder categories remain accessible. Panel and reference settings survive address restoration; ordinary filter undo/redo belongs to the common toolbar (E-299, E-300).

Panel coverage counts distinct supporting documents within the current filter. Selecting a mark additionally reports how many selected documents the companion evaluation contains. Matrix explanations distinguish absent dimension information, absent pair binding and a value outside its displayed rows or columns. A/B reference-only documents may belong to the comparison while remaining outside current B. Matrix headers wrap and stick along their own scroll axis; a saved page beyond a narrowed result is clamped to the available range. Place selection uses the actual map dimensions for a closer regional frame and retains any stronger manual zoom (E-300).

Korb. The records collected by hand across the views, as a card list in the chip pattern of the inline detail, with export to CSV, BibTeX, JSON-LD and GEXF (E-232). Every export carries the source cell of every data point.

## Accessibility

The focus ring is a two pixel accent outline with offset from `:focus-visible`, set once in `base.css` and never removed, and a skip link focuses the main content while preserving the application route and filters. Without JavaScript the page explains the requirement and links directly to project information and archival data. A failed application import offers the same links and a reload action. The application tab bar becomes operable after its handlers and initial view are ready. It is a real tab set with `role="tab"`, `aria-selected` and roving tabindex, and the Bestand heads are keyboard-operable with `aria-expanded`. A drawing is one tab stop, not one per node. Karte and Netzwerk expose a visible keyboard cursor within that drawing; arrow keys move it and Enter selects. In the Netzwerk Escape closes the selection, and the SVG carries `role="group"`, while the edges stay pointer-bound because the detail column lists the neighbours they carry as keyboard-reachable rows (E-279). The result line of the sidebar is a polite live region with `role="status"`, the loading region carries `aria-live` and `aria-busy`, and the error box is a `role="alert"` naming the requested file (E-233). Family icons are `aria-hidden` and the surrounding cell carries the name, and a section head with a generated tooltip carries an `aria-label` of its own so the tooltip text stays out of its accessible name. Under a reduced-motion preference the loading ring stands still and scrolling is not animated. Below 1200 viewport pixels the sidebar initially folds behind a labelled toggle, and the Wikidata mark takes a finger-sized target on touch widths.
